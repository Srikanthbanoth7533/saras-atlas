## 1. Evidence Quality Tiering Standard

Every factual claim, product capability, competitor feature, and mathematical formulation cited in SARAS ATLAS documentation is graded according to a three-tier evidence hierarchy:
- **Tier 1 (Primary / Certified):** Official product documentation, technical whitepapers, source code repositories, and direct API specifications.
- **Tier 2 (Secondary / Company-Reported):** Corporate press releases, published customer case studies, official marketing announcements, and conference presentations.
- **Tier 3 (Tertiary / Independent Benchmark):** Independent technology reviews, academic research papers, industry analyst publications (Gartner/Forrester), and verified commercial comparisons.
